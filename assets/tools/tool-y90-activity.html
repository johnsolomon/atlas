Y-90 radioembolization — prescribed activity
All methods use the Y-90 constant 50 Gy·kg/GBq. Glass/MIRD: A = D × m / [50 × (1−LSF)]. Resin/BSA: A = (BSA − 0.2) + Vtumour/(Vtumour+Vliver). Partition: tumour, normal-liver, and lung doses from T/N and masses. Lung dose = 50 × A × LSF / mlung; keep ≤ 30 Gy per administration (≤ 50 Gy cumulative). Residual/reflux and non-target deposition are not modelled. Educational estimate — confirm every prescription with medical physics and your institutional protocol.
