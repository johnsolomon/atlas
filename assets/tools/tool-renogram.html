Two acquisitions, read together

The perfusion (flow) study and the dynamic renogram are separate acquisitions; renovascular disease is defined by the ACE-inhibitor change, not the resting curve.

Perfusion / flow phase

A separate acquisition — rapid 1–2 s/frame over the first ~60 s after a bolus, distinct from the 10–20 s/frame dynamic renogram above. Kidney first-pass tracks the aortic bolus; blunted or asymmetric flow is nonspecific, and focally absent perfusion is a vascular emergency (arterial/venous thrombosis; in transplants quantified by the Hilson perfusion index). Driven here by each kidney's perfusion handle.

ACE-inhibitor (captopril) renography

Baseline vs post-ACEI on the same kidney. A haemodynamically significant renal-artery stenosis relies on efferent (angiotensin-II) tone to hold GFR; the ACE-inhibitor removes it, so the post-ACEI curve deteriorates — that change, not the baseline, is the test.
