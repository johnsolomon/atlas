## Who this is for

{K} This track is for the resident or attending who wants to **practice radioligand therapy**, not just know it exists. It runs from the underlying concept through the two established theranostic pairs, into dosimetry, toxicity, and the operational reality of running a program — and ends at the pipeline that is redefining the field.
