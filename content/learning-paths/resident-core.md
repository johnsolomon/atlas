## How to use this

{K} This is a **backbone**, not a syllabus to finish in a week. Work through a domain at a time — ideally aligned with your rotations — reading the anchor pages, then branch into the rest of that chapter. Each domain below lists the highest-yield starting points; the full chapter (in the left navigation) holds the depth. Test yourself with **Study mode** as you go, and keep the **High-yield** toggle on for review passes.
