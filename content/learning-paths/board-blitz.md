## How to run the sprint

{K} In the final weeks, breadth beats depth. Turn on the **High-yield** toggle (top of the navigation) so every page collapses to its key points, work through the pattern and framework pages below, then spend most of your time in **Study mode** — draw sets, self-score, and re-drill your misses. Study mode now labels each question with its topic, and can filter by domain or drill only your missed questions.
