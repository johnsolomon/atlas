From the 17-segment summed stress score (SSS) and summed rest score (SRS), this computes the summed difference score (ischemia), the percent of myocardium ischemic and scarred, and the conventional severity category. Cutoffs are the widely-cited ranges; confirm against your lab's software and normal database (see MPI Interpretation & Grading).
