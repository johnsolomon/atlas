Select the most avid residual lesion's uptake relative to the reference regions (mediastinal blood pool and liver) to get the Deauville score and its usual interpretation. Context (interim vs end-of-treatment, trial setting) determines whether a score of 3 counts as a complete metabolic response — see Lymphoma.
