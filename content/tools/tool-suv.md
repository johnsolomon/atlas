The body-weight SUV normalizes a lesion's measured activity concentration to the injected dose and the patient's weight. It is a semiquantitative surrogate for metabolism — valid for comparison only when uptake time, glucose, scanner, and reconstruction are held constant (see FDG PET in Oncology).
