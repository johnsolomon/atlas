Before Y-90 radioembolization, Tc-99m-MAA mapping estimates how much activity would shunt to the lungs. Enter the lung and liver region counts to compute the lung-shunt fraction (LSF), which — together with your dosimetry model — limits the deliverable activity to keep lung dose within accepted limits (see Y-90 Radioembolization).
