Enter the adult reference activity, the child's weight, and a minimum-activity floor to estimate a weight-scaled pediatric administered activity. This is a conservative linear convention — the EANM Dosage Card and North American consensus use non-linear, class-based multipliers, so use your local card for the definitive value (see Pediatric Nuclear Medicine & Dosing).
