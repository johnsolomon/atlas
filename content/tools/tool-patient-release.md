Under **10 CFR 35.75**, a patient may be released if the total effective dose to any other individual is unlikely to exceed **5 mSv**; **written radiation-safety instructions** are required whenever that dose is likely to exceed **1 mSv**. This calculator uses the standard **Regulatory Guide 8.39** approach — projecting the dose from the measured **dose rate at 1 metre** and the effective half-life, with a default occupancy factor of 0.25. Leave the physical half-life for a conservative upper bound, or enter a measured **effective** half-life for a patient-specific estimate.
