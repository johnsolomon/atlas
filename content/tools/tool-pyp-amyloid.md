Enter the heart and contralateral-chest ROI counts and the imaging timepoint to compute the heart-to-contralateral (H/CL) ratio, and select the Perugini visual grade. The tool returns the standard interpretation — with the mandatory reminder that a positive scan diagnoses ATTR **only** after a monoclonal protein has been excluded (see Cardiac Amyloidosis).
