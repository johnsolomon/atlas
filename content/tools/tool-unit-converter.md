## Quick Answer

Convert between the units used across nuclear medicine — **activity** (Ci ↔ Bq: mCi ↔ MBq, µCi ↔ kBq), **absorbed dose** (Gy ↔ rad), and **equivalent/effective dose** (Sv ↔ rem) — using exact definitional factors. Enter a value and its unit and every equivalent is shown at once.
